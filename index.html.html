How it works

  1. You share a fileA CSV or Excel export of your software subscriptions. Missing columns are fine.
  2. We validate and analyse itUnclear or inconsistent data is flagged to you, not silently assumed to be correct.
  3. A person reviews every findingWe check it against your needs, plan terms and renewal dates before it reaches the report.
  4. You get a report and decideWe never cancel or buy anything for you. Savings are called verified only after the lower cost shows up in your spend.

Questions

Will you cancel subscriptions for us?

No. We report what we find and recommend what to check. You approve every change.

Our data is messy. Can you still use it?

Yes. We flag missing or inconsistent fields and work with what is available. The report says what we could not confirm.

Does low usage mean a license can be removed?

Not always. That is why a person reviews each finding and why we suggest confirming with your teams before you act.
